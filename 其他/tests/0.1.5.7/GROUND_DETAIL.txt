0.1.5.7 meadow refinement

The original meadow bitmap, its roads and its ecological colour zones are unchanged.
ground_painter.gd adds permanent fine oil/gouache marks sampled from that bitmap.
Each mark has an irregular tapered silhouette, 2.1–5.8 world-pixel length, gentle
warm/cool and value variation, and low opacity. Earth-path marks are smaller and
softer than grass marks. These remain painted strokes rather than a pixel grid.

Runtime integration:
  var ground = preload("res://ground_painter.gd").new()
  add_child(ground)
  ground.setup(meadow, WORLD)
  ground.position = -camera
Remove the root's direct meadow draw. Keep its ground patches, world objects and
lighting. Update ground.position when camera changes. The module itself sets
show_behind_parent=true and z_index=-10. Include the script in app packaging.

Runtime cost:
  Single static ArrayMesh surface, built only at setup.
  55,423 strokes / 332,538 vertices for the 2,200 x 1,500 world.
  Measured first build: 43.084 ms with Godot 4.7.2 on this Mac.
  No per-frame randomisation, mesh rebuild, or per-stroke drawing calls.

Validation:
  ground_painter_check.gd passed: complete world bounds, expected brush density,
  colour opacity, one mesh surface, deterministic vertices/colours after rebuild,
  unchanged geometry after camera movement, correct draw ordering, no _process,
  and safe fallback when the base texture is unavailable.
  Sandbox user-data/log/certificate warnings did not affect these tests.
