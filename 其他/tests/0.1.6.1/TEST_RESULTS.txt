The game interface remains English. Previous releases are preserved.

Implemented
1. Continuous articulated 2D quadruped cycles, driven by actual travel distance.
   Fixed native painted body identity per heading; no reversed image sequence.
   Support and raised forward recovery are separate. Blocked/resting animals
   do not advance their walking phase; chase/flee uses the same phase pattern.
   All 40 authored heading-specific ground footprints are retained. Body, eyes,
   shadows and feet share registration. Depth-facing strides are perspective
   compressed; this is a stylized gait, not exact world-space foot locking in
   every heading. Native source PNGs are unchanged.
2. Random ecological spawn points and persistent individual temperament, goals,
   pauses, pace and escape-side choices. Passive animals flee nearby players;
   predators see, investigate scent, detour, pursue, stop/warn, then attack.
   Ground obstacles still prevent attacks through walls. NPCs independently
   roam and physically walk back to camp at dusk. Menus freeze complete state.
3. Continuous dawn/noon/evening/dusk/midnight/before-dawn exposure and warmth.
   Torch: 2 branches + 2 fibers, field-craft in C; T toggles it. 120 lit seconds,
   160-world-unit / 5 m outer radius, soft falloff. Unlit time saves fuel.
   Drop/pickup preserves remaining fuel. The right-hand weapon stays equipped.
   Camp remains illuminated; midnight elsewhere retains dense 98% dark fog.
4. Actual wolves' red and bears' green eye landmarks glow in darkness, blink,
   obey distance/ground-obstacle visibility, and fade in light. Rear-facing
   animals show no eyes. No decorative phantom enemies are generated.

The initial native night/torch tests exposed precision loss in tiny world-space
pigment polygons. Final rendering triangulates local coordinates first and
submits explicit mesh triangles with translation afterward. Affected original
regressions were rerun without weakening assertions and now pass.

Performance samples (headless, this machine, not an FPS guarantee)
Lighting mesh build mean ~0.99 ms. Cold species navigation cache builds wolf
~3.4 ms / bear ~4.1 ms, then is reused until the map navigation revision changes.
