User correction
Keep the attractive original 0.1.6 painted animal art, including legs, fur and
paws, while retaining a complete forward walking cycle. The flat procedural
leg shapes from 0.1.6.1 were rejected. No new bitmap artwork was generated.

Implementation
All five original PNG atlases remain unchanged. Moving animals use their own
original painted leg pixels in a continuous UV skin mesh; resting animals show
the complete original painting. Original paw size, silhouette, texture and
native resolution are retained. Four-pixel mesh spacing controls deformation,
not texture resolution. Phase still advances in order from actual travel.

The first two-bone prototype folded painted triangles and opened cut seams.
Those defects were actually reproduced in Canvas replays and independent
mesh checks. The final field is piecewise affine on the source grid, with a
positive-Jacobian bound. Original-paint edge underlaps close opaque attachment
cuts. Two-native-pixel geometric mask guards collect painted fur/toe edges
instead of leaving fixed old-paw fragments behind. Thirty-one moving-only
registration biases retain existing physical ground envelopes. No inverted
painted triangles are discarded and no flat-colour patches hide the defects.
