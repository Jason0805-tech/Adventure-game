Forest Wanderer 0.1.6 - wildlife simulation acceptance

Runtime files:
  wildlife.gd - rendering-independent ecological placement, states, combat and ground collision
  wildlife_data.gd - species stats, habitat rectangles, period restrictions and safety values

Species / activity / cap / behavior:
  Meadow rabbit / day / 4 / wanders, flees from nearby player
  Woodland deer / day / 3 / wanders, flees from farther away
  Red fox / day and dusk / 2 / woodland wander and player avoidance
  Night wolf / night / 3 / patrol, visible-target chase, stopped telegraph, bite, recovery
  Forest bear / night / 1 / slower patrol/chase, longer swipe telegraph and recovery

The day/dusk/night rules are game design, not real-world animal-behavior claims.
Period changes remove wrong-time species and colliders immediately, even with zero delta.
Regular simulation pauses stop behavior, attack timers and respawn timers.
Normal seed 0 randomizes; supplied nonzero seeds reproduce ecology.

Spawn placement uses species-specific ground capsules against all real ground contours,
including water/shore stones. It avoids the 160-unit camp safe zone, 84-unit gate-passage
area, 90-unit NPC start radius and other animal feet. Day species start at least 170 units
from the player; night predators start at least 360 units away. A navigation route from
the player is required, rejecting disconnected scenery pockets. Respawn checks every
28 simulation seconds and never exceed the species caps. No new loot items are introduced.

Wolf: 95 chase speed, 0.65-second stopped locked-aim warning, 12 damage, 1.30-second recovery.
Bear: 68 chase speed, 0.95-second stopped locked-aim warning, 22 damage, 1.85-second recovery.
Both can be outrun by the existing 108-unit/second player. All predators have 3-second
spawn grace. Shared 1.10-second player hurt grace prevents simultaneous pack damage.
Weapon hits interrupt warnings, enter recovery and enforce per-animal 0.30-second hit grace.
Ground walls block sight and melee hits. The host wildlife_damage callback honors wards.
Animals never enter camp; entering camp cancels a pursuing predator's attack.

  wildlife.update(game, delta, "day"|"dusk"|"night", enabled)
