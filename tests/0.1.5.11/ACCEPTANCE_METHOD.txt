Independent source ground landmarks are selected from native cropped PNGs,
without opening collision_profiles.json to generate expected coordinates.
They test solid ground bases and explicit free upper-image areas. All water and
neighbor stones are removed for each stone's own tests, so water cannot hide a
missing rock body. Upper roofs, trunk forks, tabletops and arch lintels must not
become physical walls. Field branch/fiber plants and spent remnants are soft.
A point marked free may be near real wood; such source-center-only samples do
not claim a full28×14bootcapsule fits there. All distant upper samples also test
full boots and real motion across the object at that elevation.

The first five root points were mistakenly selected on green foreground leaves
at small source previews. Source4× crops exposed this; the exact original points
remain as foliage center-negative tests, and new visibly bare wood points were
added. Original failure logs/reports remain alongside final output. This was
source annotation correction, not changing expectations to match polygons.

includes camp, behind the elevated cabin roof,3pondapproaches,4quadrants,
