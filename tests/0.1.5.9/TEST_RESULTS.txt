RENDERING CHANGES
- Cache vertical source-texture bands using each column's own lower solid contour.
- Once an actor is locally in front, keep its complete body/hand span coherent across the nearby bands, instead of cutting an arm off at a deeper adjacent stair column. This rule also applies to NPCs and mirrored scenery.
- Preserve real behind-structure ordering and live-tree canopy fading. The gate's open center keeps its overhead header depth.
- Partition prepared pond pixels into base water/ground and 17 separate shore-rock layers. Source alpha is exclusive and exactly conserved at each source pixel.
- Collision JSON and every original asset file are byte-identical to 0.1.5.8. Ground/static scenery keep 2-world-unit texel density; avatar, grip, speed and day length are preserved.
