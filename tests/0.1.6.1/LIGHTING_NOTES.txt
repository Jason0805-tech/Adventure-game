Forest Wanderer 0.1.6.1 — lighting implementation / reproducible checks

Source: output/adventure game 0.1.6.1/world_lighting.gd
Native Canvas ArrayMesh, indexed triangles and smoothly interpolated vertex alpha.
The 18-screen-pixel lighting grid changes illumination only. No source art is
resized, downsampled or rewritten. Native 1100x720 mesh build measured 0.99 ms
mean across 100 changing player positions on this machine, headless Godot.

Scale: 32 world units = 1 metre. A torch has a 160-world-unit outer radius,
bright centre to 60.8 units and a smooth falloff to darkness at five metres.
No torch means 98% fog at midnight outside camp. At most a barely visible
22-unit pool around one's boots prevents losing the player entirely; it reveals
no ground at 25 units. Camp's safe radius160 remains visibly lit inside its
205-unit soft campfire falloff. Torch item, crafting, fuel, toggling and visible
hand prop are integrated in main.gd by the root agent.

Lighting phase continuity:
 dawn .36 darkness + .13 warmth -> clear noon0 -> evening .52 +.16warmth
 -> 6-second dusk .95 -> midnight .98 -> final16seconds sunrise -> dawn .36.
 Night result and next day's first frame use the same dawn values. The maximum
 darkness change per1/60second over a complete day+dusk+night is .001792.
 All animation/blink timing reads the paused simulation elapsed time.

Eye glows are attached to actual wolf/bear entries and their authored native
head landmarks. Gait.eye_offsets supplies exact body bob registration. Wolves
are muted red; bears muted green. Rear views correctly show no eyes; side views
show one. The effect fades when exposed by torch/camp illumination. It is limited
to300world units, fades past235, and never invents animals in empty space.
Active solid ground polygons block the sight segment; water does not. Elevated
leaf canopies are not separate LOS walls, while the normal animal/body rendering
still uses the game's depth layers. Blinks are staggered per UID.
