# v0.3.0 重画 — 生图提示词（给 ChatGPT）

目标：把河谷和废墟的 **13 张图全部重画**，换成和森林一样的画风：**简约、油画/水粉、看得见笔触、轻松愉快**。

## 上一版为什么丑（新提示词专门针对这几点）

| 问题 | 上一版 | 这次怎么改 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 太写实、像 3D 渲染 | 石柱、雕像、柳树都有很细的裂纹、高光、立体光影 | 明确要求"由大块方笔触拼出来"，每个物件只用 3–4 个明暗，禁止 3D/写实 |
| 细节太碎 | 碎石堆里几十块小石头、草叶一根一根 | 要求"大形状、少细节"，并给出数量（例如碎石堆只要 4–6 块） |
| 颜色发灰、发闷 | 灰米色石头、黄褐色草 | 指定和森林一样的调色板：鲜绿、蓝绿阴影、沙黄、淡紫灰石头 |
| 和森林不搭 | 物件底下没有森林里那种叶丛 | 每个物件底部加一小圈和参考图一样的方块叶丛 |
| 地面糊、像有雾 | 石板地一层白雾、颗粒噪点 | 要求"颜色饱满、不透明、边缘清楚，没有雾和噪点" |
| 尺寸要求无效 | 要 2400×1600，GPT 根本给不了 | 只要 GPT 能给的 **横版 3:2（1536×1024）** |

## 怎么用

1. **开一个新的 ChatGPT 对话**（别在上次生成 0.3.0 的对话里继续，它会沿用旧画风）。
2. 先发下面的 **第 0 步：定风格**，附上参考图 ref1–ref4。等它回复 ready。
3. 然后**一次只发一张图的提示词**，每次都附上那张图标注的参考图（在 `参考图/` 文件夹里，全部来自 0.2.0 之前的版本）。
4. **建议先做图 8（废墟物件）当试验**——这张上次最丑，最能看出风格对不对。生成后先发给我看，我根据效果调整提示词，再做其他的。
5. 物件图要**透明背景**。如果 GPT 给了白底或别的底色，就让它"用纯品红 #FF00FF 纯色背景重画"，我来抠。（不要白底：会和米白色高光混在一起抠不干净。）
6. 地面贴图不用担心左右接不上——拿到后我会处理成无缝的。
7. 保存到这个文件夹 `素材整理/区域_0.3.0_重画/`，文件名和下面一样。
8. 不满意就跟它说哪里不对（或者直接告诉我，我来写修改指令）。

## 第 0 步：定风格（附 ref1、ref2、ref3、ref4）

```
I am making art for a cozy 2D top-down exploration game. The attached images are the ART STYLE TARGET. Every image I ask for in this chat must match them exactly in style. Please study them and remember this style:

- Simple hand-painted gouache / oil painting look with clearly visible brush strokes.
- Everything is built from short, chunky, slightly square brush dabs that overlap, like painting with a small flat brush or palette knife. Edges of shapes are broken and jagged from the dabs, never smooth or vector-clean.
- Flat colour masses. Each object uses only 3 or 4 tones: a light warm top, a mid tone and a cool teal-green shadow. No gradients, no soft airbrush shading.
- Bright, fresh, cheerful storybook palette: fresh green and yellow-green grass, deep teal-green shadows, warm sandy ochre, soft lavender-grey stone, warm brown wood, cream highlights. Gentle sunny daylight from the upper left.
- Very simple: big readable shapes, very few details, nothing tiny or fiddly. Relaxed, light-hearted and friendly.
- No black outlines, no ink lines, no realistic or 3D-rendered look, no glossy highlights, no photo texture, no pixel art, no gloomy dark mood.

Do not generate an image yet. Just reply "ready".
```

## 常用修改指令（不满意时直接发给 ChatGPT）

- 太写实 / 像 3D：`Too realistic. Make it much simpler and flatter: fewer details, bigger chunky brush dabs, only 3-4 flat tones per object, exactly like the attached reference sprites.`
- 颜色发灰：`Too grey and dull. Use the brighter, fresher, more cheerful colours of the reference: fresh greens, teal shadows, lavender-grey stone, warm cream highlights.`
- 笔触太细：`The brush strokes are too fine and smooth. Use bigger, chunkier, slightly square dabs with broken jagged edges, like the reference.`
- 背景不透明：`Same image, but on a flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, no gradient, no shadow.`
- 物件粘在一起：`Keep every object fully separate with wide empty space between them. Nothing may touch or overlap.`
- 地面有雾 / 糊：`Remove the haze. Clean, solid, opaque colours with crisp brush edges, no noise, no fog.`
