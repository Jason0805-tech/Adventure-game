# v0.3.0 补充图 — 生图提示词（3 张）

v0.3.0-alpha.1 里还在用临时美术的三样东西：河谷和废墟的**土路**、废墟里的**沉井**（Sunken Well）、废墟入口的**倒塌石门**（Fallen Gate）。

## 怎么生成

1. 和上次一样，一张一张生成，每次都附上参考图：
   - `其他/tests/art-previews/001/oil-cartoon-scene.png`（整体画风）
   - 图 1 附：`output/0.3.X/adventure game 0.3.0.1/assets/meadow.png`（森林地面里的土路画法）
   - 图 2、图 3 附：`素材整理/区域_0.3.0/ruins-props.png`（上次生成的废墟物件，让石头的颜色和画法一致）
2. 图 1 是地面贴图，铺满整张图、不要透明；图 2、图 3 要**背景透明的 PNG**（不支持透明就用纯色背景）。
3. 放进 `素材整理/区域_0.3.0/`，用下面写的文件名。

---

## 图 1：土路 → `ground-path.png`

```
Use case: production game ground texture. The attached meadow image is the STYLE TARGET: match the warm ochre DIRT ROAD painted in it (not the grass) — the same loose, broad, hand-painted oil/gouache cartoon brushwork, irregular short dabs and soft overcast light.

Paint ONE square, full-bleed, seamless tileable ground texture of that trodden DIRT PATH seen straight top-down: warm pale ochre and sandy earth with slightly darker worn patches, a few faint wheel or foot ruts running left to right, scattered tiny pebbles. The left edge must continue seamlessly into the right edge and the top into the bottom. Even density, no focal point, no grass border, no lighting gradient.

Ground only: no grass edges, no stones bigger than pebbles, no plants, no shadows, no objects, no text, no frame. Not realism, not 3D, no photo texture. Largest square size available.
```

## 图 2：沉井 → `ruin-well.png`

```
Use case: production game sprite. Attached images are STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match the stone colours, moss and painting style of the attached ruins sheet and the loose oil/gouache cartoon brushwork of the reference scene; no black outlines.

ONE isolated object, centered on a 1200 x 1200 transparent canvas, contained in the central 70 percent, base at the bottom: an OLD SUNKEN WELL in the ruins — a knee-high round ring of weathered grey-blue carved stones, partly crumbled on one side, a broken wooden crossbeam and a short frayed rope, dark water deep inside, moss and dry grass tufts at the base. A faint spiral rune carved on one stone. Quiet and a little eerie, not scary.

Camera: fixed orthographic elevated top-down 2D game view about 55-65 degrees looking down, matching the reference; you can see into the well opening. A few broad irregular paint masses, sparse details. No text, labels, frame, ground disc, cast shadow, scenery or background. Real transparent alpha background.
```
