Create a 4 columns x 4 rows sheet of 16 ARMLESS AND LEGLESS traveler TORSO cutouts, on real transparent alpha. Each cutout contains ONLY head, blue beanie + gold pompom, tiny rough dot eyes, orange hair, mustard coat body down to its rounded bottom hem, and olive backpack + bedroll. No arms, sleeves hanging outside body, hands, fingers, legs, trousers, boots, weapon, shadows, backdrop, paper, text, labels or grid lines. Arms and legs will be attached by the game animation rig; adding any here creates duplicate limbs. The yellow coat is a compact body garment with shoulders ready for separate sleeve attachment, not a long dress.

Exact same cozy 2D oil/gouache style and proportions as image 1: broad irregular opaque brush masses, few features, matte flat paint, tiny rough eyes, warm ochre coat, teal-blue hat, olive pack. NOT a 3D model, realistic volume, plastic, smooth shading or fine realistic texture. Image 2 must be REPAINTED in image 1's art style, do not retain its rendering appearance. No shiny outlines or glow.

Follow the exact yaw in each corresponding cell of image 2. Left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
0,30,45,60 / 90,120,135,150 / 180,210,225,240 / 270,300,315,330.
0 front face; 90 profile facing RIGHT; 180 back; 270 profile facing LEFT.
120,135,150 are rear views turned to screen RIGHT, diminishing cheek on RIGHT, not left.
210,225,240 are rear views turned to screen LEFT, cheek on LEFT, not right.
30 and 330 are shallow turns near front; 45 and315 diagonals; 60 and300 approach profiles.
Head, torso, coat and backpack turn together, keep angle changes subtle and true, never accidentally reverse rear views.

Sixteen evenly spaced complete cutouts at equal scale. Keep every torso fully isolated within its own cell with generous transparent margins. Pompom/face/coat all connected as one character cutout; preserve rough hand-painted edges and actual alpha. No baked ground shadow or foot circle. No new costume, accessories or design changes.
