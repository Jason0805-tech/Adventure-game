Forest Wanderer — The Old Beacon

A painted woodland survival adventure. You are lost in a deep forest with nothing but a camp and an old ranger named Rowan. Gather what you need, keep yourself fed, guard your mind against the dark, and find three lost parts to light the old beacon and escape the forest.

NEW IN v0.3.0-alpha.3 — REPAINTED VALLEY AND RUINS
The River Valley and the Fallen Ruins have been completely repainted to match the forest: the same chunky, cheerful brushwork and bright colours. New ground for the meadows, riverbanks, river, fords, flagstones, dry grass and dirt roads; new ruins, willows, reeds, boulders, driftwood, the ferry post, bridge, dock, Sunken Well and Fallen Gate; and new item icons. The ground no longer shows mirrored, repeating patterns.

FROM v0.3.0 — WORLD EXPANSION
The forest is no longer the whole world. The map is almost three times bigger, with three regions to explore:
- Whispering Woods: the home forest and your camp. Timber, berries and iron ore; wolves at night.
- River Valley (east): a wide river you can only cross at the old bridge or two shallow fords. Wading is slow and the splashing carries, and bears like the riverbanks. Gather reeds and fish the river for trout.
- Fallen Ruins (south): a silent stone town. The ruins drain your sanity even by day, and deep inside, shadows walk in daylight. Break the rubble for ancient stone and old bronze; carve a rune charm to keep the whispers away.

The three beacon parts now lie in three different regions, and the repair needs ancient stone from the ruins, so you will have to brave all of them.

Also new: 7 landmarks, 2 more supply caches, a journal grouped by region, and a world map that shows all three regions.
The valley and ruins have their own painted ground, dirt roads, ruins, the Fallen Gate you walk through, the Sunken Well, willows, reeds, the bridge and new item icons.

FROM v0.2.0 — NIGHT SURVIVAL 2.0
Every night is different now. At dusk the game tells you what tonight brings:
- Howling Pack: an extra wolf joins, and the pack tracks your trail.
- Thick Fog: the dark closes in. You see less, they see less, and your mind frays faster.
- Restless Bear: a bear roams wide, follows your trail and charges more often.
- Blood Moon (from night 3): a red sky, faster sanity loss, and shadows that walk sooner.

Wolves now hunt as a pack. One goes for you while another swings around your side, and the rest circle and wait for their turn. Stand near the fire and they lose their nerve.

Lighting the beacon starts THE LAST WATCH. Defend it for 60 seconds while waves of wolves come out of the dark — and on later nights, a bear.

FROM v0.1.9 — SMARTER WILDLIFE
- Predators follow your scent trail at night — the places you walked, not where you stand.
- Chopping, mining, fighting and sprinting make noise that draws nearby wolves.
- A lit torch keeps your mind steady but makes curious wolves come closer.
- Startle one deer and the whole herd runs.

FEATURES
- Hand-painted forest with day and night, campfire light and a torch.
- Hunger, health and sanity to manage.
- Gather wood, stone, ore and food; craft tools and weapons at the workbench.
- Journal and map with fog of war, 15 landmarks and 6 hidden supply caches across three regions.
- Sprint with stamina.
- Plays on desktop and on phones (landscape).

CONTROLS (desktop)
WASD — move · Shift — sprint · E — gather / talk · Space or click — use item
1–6 — hotbar · B — bag · C — craft · H — heal · T — torch · M — map · J — journal

CONTROLS (phone)
Turn your phone sideways. Left stick to move; buttons for Run, Gather, Use, Bag, Craft, Heal, Torch and Map.
